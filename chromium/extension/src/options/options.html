Stream and share without leaking your details

Cloak Desk hides email addresses, phone numbers, card numbers, IDs and API keys on every web page, so viewers of your stream or screen share never see them. You still see everything you type. It's off until you turn it on.

  3. 3

Protection

Hide sensitive details
Off. Turn on before you go live.
When to hide
Streaming apps outside the browser can't be detected, so use Always for OBS and desktop apps.
Keyboard shortcut
Alt+Shift+H turns protection on or off

What to hide

Found anywhere in a page: text, tables, chat, code, editors and inside web components. Cards, IBANs and Aadhaar numbers are checksum-verified.

Your own words

Your name, home address, company, client names, usernames… Matched anywhere on a page, ignoring upper and lower case.

Tip: select text on any page, right-click and choose Always hide “…”.

Advanced: patterns (regular expressions)

JavaScript syntax. Case-insensitive unless written as /…/ without the i flag.

Fields, titles & alerts

Hide what's typed in sensitive fields
Email, phone, card, address, password and token fields show dots, even after a “show password” click.
Clean tab titles
“Inbox – you@gmail.com – Gmail” becomes “Inbox – ••••• – Gmail” in the tab strip.
Hide notification previews
Chat and mail pop-ups from open sites show only the site name, not the message.
Count on the toolbar button
Shows how many details are hidden on the current page.

Privacy & limits

Nothing leaves your computer

No servers, no analytics, no accounts, and no network code at all. The popup, this page and the background worker are also blocked from the network by the extension's security policy. Settings stay on this device and are never synced.

Pages can't read your list

Your words and patterns stay inside the extension. Pages are never changed: masks are painted on top, so sites keep working and can't see what was hidden.

Why “read and change all sites”

To find sensitive text on whatever page you show. Cloak Desk only reads text to cover it; it never stores, logs or sends page content.

What it can't hide
  • Chrome's own UI: address bar, autofill pop-ups, bookmarks, the Chrome Web Store and chrome:// pages.
  • Text inside images, videos, PDFs and canvas apps (Google Docs, Figma).
  • Matches highlighted by Chrome's Find bar (Ctrl+F).
  • Other apps on your desktop. For those, use Cloak Desk for Windows.
