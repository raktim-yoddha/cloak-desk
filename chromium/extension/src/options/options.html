General

Off

Keyboard shortcut

Turns protection on or off from any tab.

When to hide

Detection

Found in any text on a page, including chats, tables, code, editors and embedded frames. Cards, IBANs and Aadhaar numbers are checksum-verified, so ordinary numbers aren't covered.

Your words

Your name, home address, company or client names. Matched anywhere, in any letter case.

You can also select text on any page, right-click, and choose “Always hide”.

Patterns

JavaScript regular expressions. Without slashes, matching ignores letter case.

More places

Form fields

Email, phone, card, address, password and token fields show dots, even after “show password”.

Tab titles

“Inbox – you@gmail.com” becomes “Inbox – •••••” in the tab strip.

Notifications

Pop-ups from open sites show the site name instead of the message.

Count on toolbar button

How many details are covered on the current page.

Sites

The whole page is replaced while protection is on. Good for password managers and banking.

Subdomains are included.

Privacy

Nothing leaves your computer. There are no servers, accounts or analytics, and no network code; the extension's own pages are also blocked from the network by its security policy. Settings, including your words, stay on this device and are never synced.

Chrome describes the site access as “read and change all your data”. Cloak Desk needs it to find sensitive text on whatever page you show. It reads text only to cover it, never stores or sends it, and never changes the page itself: covers are painted on top.

What it can't cover

  • Chrome's own interface: the address bar, autofill suggestions, bookmarks, chrome:// pages and the Web Store.
  • Text inside images, video, PDFs and canvas-drawn apps such as Google Docs and Figma.
  • Matches highlighted by Find (Ctrl+F).
  • Other apps on your desktop. Cloak Desk for Windows covers those.

Data

A JSON file with your words and sites. Keep it private.

Import settings

Restores the defaults and clears your words and sites.
